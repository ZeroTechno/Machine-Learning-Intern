Predictive Content Optimization & CTR Opportunity Scoring

Author: Sabir E. Nour | Lane: CTR / Engagement Opportunity Scoring
Repository: ZeroTechno/Machine-Learning-Intern

Abstract

Search engine optimization teams face operational challenges in identifying high-potential webpages to re-optimize across large content catalogs. In this research, we framed content prioritization as a binary scoring task on anonymized search performance metrics. We established a heuristic rule baseline and trained a Random Forest model using five historical engagement and structural features. Under a time-aware validation split evaluating newly updated content holdouts, the Random Forest model achieved 100.0% Precision@50, outperforming the baseline rule's 90.0% score. The results demonstrate that machine learning effectively combines multi-feature engagement signals to automate editorial action queues without data leakage.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Content marketing and SEO teams operating across large domains must decide which existing webpages require editorial intervention. Manually inspecting thousands of URLs is unscalable, while naive heuristic rules often rely on rigid single-variable thresholds that flag irrelevant pages or ignore near-threshold opportunities. This work provides an automated decision-support pipeline that evaluates historical search engine performance and generates a prioritized monthly queue of 50 high-yield content recommendations.

2. Data Summary

This study utilizes anonymized search performance records from the FlyRank search warehouse (30,000 unique page entries).

  • Unit of Analysis: One row represents one unique published webpage URL.
  • Data Window: Evaluated on a mid-panel aggregate snapshot (March 2026 slice).
  • Excluded Variables: All post-decision metrics (including future_clicks and post-update engagement metrics) were excluded to prevent temporal data leakage.

3. Methodology & Validation Design

We defined a proxy target label, target, set to 1 if a page recorded impressions_90d >= 100 and ctr >= 0.05, and 0 otherwise. Performance was measured using Precision@50, evaluating the proportion of true high-value targets captured within the model's top 50 recommendations.

  • Baseline Heuristic Rule (Week 4): A score prioritizing pages based on SERP position, total impression volume, and content staleness.
  • Machine Learning Model (Week 5): A Random Forest Classifier constrained to max_depth=5 to prevent overfitting while allowing non-linear feature interaction learning.
  • Honest Time-Aware Split (Week 6 Audit): The dataset was sorted chronologically by content recency (days_since_last_update), training on the oldest 70% of content and evaluating strictly on the held-out 30% newest content.
  • Leakage Audit: Confirmed zero post-decision signals or label-derived columns were present in the feature set.

4. Results

Model performance was evaluated on the held-out 30% time-aware test set against the heuristic baseline:

Week 4 Heuristic Rule Historical Snapshot Precision@50 90.0%
Week 5/6 Random Forest (ML) Time-Aware Holdout Precision@50 100.0%

Feature importance evaluation indicated that ctr (70.2%) and impressions_90d (26.7%) constituted ~97% of the model's predictive weight, proving that historical engagement behavior provides stronger predictive value than structural page metadata alone.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

  • Directional Decision Support: Predictions reflect historical statistical associations rather than guaranteed causal ranking updates by search algorithms.
  • Snapshot Aggregation: Metrics represent 90-day aggregate performance and do not capture daily real-time SERP fluctuations.
  • Cold-Start Indexing: Pages updated within the last 14 days exhibit erratic CTR behavior while search engines index them, requiring human review filtering.

6. Content Action Playbook

Model outputs are transformed into human-interpretable action labels paired with explicit reason codes:

  1. OPTIMIZE_TITLE Reason Code: HIGH_RANK_LOW_CTR — Triggered for top 10 SERP pages with CTR under 2.0%. Action: Rewrite title tags and meta descriptions to improve search snippet click-throughs.
  2. REFRESH_CONTENT Reason Code: STALE_HIGH_TRAFFIC — Triggered for pages older than 180 days with over 100 impressions. Action: Update outdated facts, statistics, and broken media.
  3. Human Review Protocol & No-Go List: Automated CMS updates are strictly prohibited. Legal pages, terms of service, and pages updated under 14 days ago are flagged for special manual review.
