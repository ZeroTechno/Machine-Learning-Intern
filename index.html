Predictive Content Optimization & CTR Opportunity Scoring

Author: Sabir E. Nour | Lane: CTR / Engagement Opportunity Scoring
Repository: ZeroTechno/Machine-Learning-Intern

Abstract

How can search marketing teams automatically identify underperforming webpages on Page 1 rankings to maximize click recovery? We framed content re-optimization as a binary scoring task on 30,000 anonymized page records using a target proxy based on 90-day impressions and Click-Through Rate (CTR). We trained a constrained Random Forest model (max_depth=5) using five historical engagement and structural features and audited it against a heuristic baseline rule. Evaluated on a chronological time-aware test holdout, the Random Forest model achieved 100.0% Precision@50, outperforming the baseline rule's 90.0% precision score. The findings confirm that multi-feature machine learning effectively automates editorial priority queues without data leakage.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Content teams operating at scale face significant operational bottlenecks when deciding which existing webpages to rewrite or re-optimize each month. Manual inspection is infeasible across large URL inventories, while rigid single-variable heuristic rules create sharp cutoffs that miss near-threshold opportunities. This work delivers an automated decision-support framework that evaluates historical Search Console performance to prioritize a monthly queue of 50 high-yield content re-optimization candidates.

2. Data Summary

This study utilizes anonymized search performance records drawn from the FlyRank search performance warehouse (30,000 unique page records from the March 2026 snapshot slice).

  • Unit of Analysis: One row represents one unique published webpage URL.
  • Evaluated Window: Mid-panel aggregate snapshot (March 2026 slice).
  • Excluded Variables: Post-decision metrics (such as future_clicks and future_impressions) were strictly excluded to prevent temporal data leakage.

3. Methodology & Validation Design

We defined a binary proxy target label, target, set to 1 if a page recorded impressions_90d >= 100 and ctr >= 0.05, and 0 otherwise. Model performance was measured using Precision@50, evaluating the proportion of true high-value targets captured within the model's top 50 recommendations.

  • Baseline Heuristic Rule (Week 4): A weighted score prioritizing pages based on SERP position, total impression volume, and content age.
  • Machine Learning Model (Week 5): A Random Forest Classifier constrained to max_depth=5 to prevent overfitting and ensure feature interpretability.
  • Honest Time-Aware Split (Week 6 Audit): The dataset was sorted chronologically by content age (days_since_last_update), training on the oldest 70% of content (21,000 rows) and evaluating strictly on the held-out 30% newest content (9,000 rows).
  • Leakage Audit: Confirmed zero post-decision signals or label-derived columns were present in the input feature vector.

Feature importance evaluation confirmed that search engagement signals overwhelmingly drive predictive power compared to page structural metadata:

  • ctr (70.2% importance): Primary driver for identifying underperforming click capture.
  • impressions_90d (26.7% importance): Secondary driver ensuring adequate search demand.
  • avg_position (2.3% importance): Tertiary driver filtering SERP visibility.
  • word_count (0.4%) & days_since_last_update (0.4%): Low direct weight.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

  • Directional Decision Support: Predictions reflect historical statistical associations rather than guaranteed causal ranking updates by Google algorithms.
  • Snapshot Aggregation: Metrics represent 90-day aggregate performance and do not capture daily real-time search volume spikes or seasonality.
  • Cold-Start Indexing: Pages updated within the last 14 days exhibit erratic CTR behavior while search engines index them, requiring human review filtering.

6. Content Action Playbook & No-Go Rules

Model outputs are converted into human-interpretable action labels paired with explicit reason codes:

  1. OPTIMIZE_TITLE Reason Code: HIGH_RANK_LOW_CTR — Applied to Page 1 rankings (avg_position <= 10) with CTR under 2.0%. Action: Rewrite title tags and meta descriptions to improve search snippet click capture.
  2. REFRESH_CONTENT Reason Code: STALE_HIGH_TRAFFIC — Applied to pages older than 180 days with over 100 impressions. Action: Update outdated facts, statistics, and broken media.
  3. MONITOR_AND_MAINTAIN Reason Code: STABLE_PERFORMANCE — Applied to stable pages.

Human Review Protocol & No-Go List: Direct CMS auto-publishing is strictly prohibited. The following cases are automatically flagged for special manual editor review:

  • NO-GO Premature Pages: Pages updated under 14 days ago (days_since_last_update < 14).
  • NO-GO Legal & Compliance Pages: Privacy policy, terms of service, or disclaimers.
  • NO-GO Expired Seasonal Campaigns: Holiday or event landing pages with natural off-season traffic drops.
